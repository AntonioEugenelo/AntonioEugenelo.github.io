About

I am a DPhil candidate in Economics at the University of Oxford and a member of Nuffield College, where I previously completed the MPhil in Economics with Distinction.

I work in macroeconomics, mainly on monetary and fiscal policy. My current research asks how the path an economy is already on—after one shock has hit and before it has settled—changes the transmission of the next shock and the appropriate policy response to it. A second line of work studies how sectoral heterogeneity shapes the optimal mix of fiscal and monetary stabilisation.

Before Oxford I studied International Economics and Finance at Bocconi University, with an exchange semester at Princeton. I have worked as a research assistant at the European Central Bank, on trade policy and production networks, at lavoce.info, and at Bocconi’s Sovereign Investment Lab.
